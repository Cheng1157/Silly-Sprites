A digital card game

Silly Sprites

Name them. Remember them. Shout first!

How to play

  1. Take turns tapping to flip the next card.
  2. New creature? Give it a silly name and say it loud.
  3. Seen before? Be first to shout its name!
  4. Each time you are the first, write down a tally.
  5. After card 60, the most tallies wins.

12 creatures, 5 copies each, 60 cards. Tap the right side of the screen for the next card and the left side to go back. You can also swipe.
